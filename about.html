What is The ShuleShift?

Finding a school with space in Kenya is hard. Parents walk from school to school, calling and asking "Do you have space?"

ShuleShift makes it easy. We list schools in ALL 47 Counties with their fees, location, available spaces, photos, and WhatsApp - all in one place.
